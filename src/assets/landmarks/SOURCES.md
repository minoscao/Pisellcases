[CC BY 3.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Original GLBs are retained. Runtime adaptations: normalization, symbolic display scale, material changes, torch accent, architectural illumination, kangaroo hopping, toucan turning, and a three-pyramid composition. Architecture anchors identify their locations; the pagoda and animals are regional motifs, not surveyed landmarks or company projects. Credits are also available from the app menu.

Asset generation evaluation: `TRIPO_API_KEY=MISSING`. No generation or rigging task was submitted. The generator skill and its API notes were read; Tripo integration and rigging references were not applicable because all assets are sourced GLBs, animated locally without generated skeletons.
